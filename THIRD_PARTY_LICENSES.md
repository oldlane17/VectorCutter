# Third-Party Licenses

VectorCutter is 100% client-side, vanilla JavaScript (ES6+), with no telemetry and MIT-compatible.
The following third-party fonts, tools, and libraries are referenced or integrated into the application:

---

### MathJax (v3)
- **Purpose:** LaTeX equation rendering and SVG vector glyph conversion.
- **License:** Apache License 2.0
- **Website:** https://www.mathjax.org/
